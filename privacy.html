Privacy notice

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This notice covers Champ Game Zone at basketballchamp123.github.io, including the Two in a Row basketball game. The site is operated by basketballchamp123 and is intended for a general audience.

Current status
The site does not currently display ads or use Google Analytics. We are preparing to use Google AdSense; the advertising information below explains that planned feature.

Playing the games

You do not need an account to play. The current game keeps your score, level, team selection and jersey number in the browser while the page is open. The game does not send these values to us or save them in cookies or browser storage for later visits. Reloading the game starts a new session.

The game uses your browser’s audio and text-to-speech features for sound effects and announcements. You can turn sound off using the game’s sound control.

Hosting and fonts

GitHub Pages hosts the site. When you visit, GitHub receives technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address. GitHub’s handling of this information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The home page and game load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to request those files, which gives Google your IP address and information needed to deliver the fonts. Google states that the Fonts Web API is not used to create user profiles or for advertising. See Google Fonts privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Planned advertising

When Google AdSense is enabled, Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Google’s advertising cookies allow it and its partners to personalize ads based on visits to this site and other websites.

You can manage Google personalized advertising through My Ad Center. Some other vendors offer choices at YourAdChoices. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising and its consent controls are not active yet. Before enabling ads, we will update this notice to describe the active advertising partners and the privacy choices available on the site.

Contact and privacy requests

For questions or requests about your information, email basketballchamp@gmail.com. If you email us, your email address and message will be available to us and our email provider so we can handle your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your question.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or object to certain uses. You can contact us about information you have provided directly. For information handled independently by GitHub or Google, their privacy notices explain their processes and contact options.

Changes

We will update this page when our games, services or privacy practices change. The date above shows the latest update.
